Commercialization

From research to supported deployment.

Distinct streams, kept separate on purpose.

Proprietary research, applied engineering, and commercial services are different things at different levels of maturity. We keep them clearly distinguished so no one line borrows credibility from another, and so research-stage work is never presented as a finished product.

01

Proprietary technology commercialization

The STRIX research-to-market strategy: proposed institutional deployments, potential enterprise licensing, and managed research infrastructure. These are proposed pathways, contingent on documented technical feasibility and demonstrated commercial need.

02

Applied computational technologies

Complementary analytical systems and engineering initiatives — including C-Engine and BLKPVNTHR.OS — that apply the company's computational engineering to specific domains.

03

Commercial engineering services

PantherByte, the company's available-now service line: managed digital infrastructure, software engineering, workflow automation, integrations, and ongoing technical support.
