Engineering-led from the beginning.

BLKPVNTHR LLC was founded by Asmaa Abdul-Amin, a Maryland software engineer whose prior engineering experience includes building production software supporting NASA and Johns Hopkins Applied Physics Laboratory mission platforms — spanning database-driven applications, administrative systems, data pipelines, APIs, dashboards, and research-oriented software.

The company pairs that engineering discipline with a research agenda in trustworthy computational intelligence. It is deliberately narrow: it takes on work where correctness matters more than speed, and where a conclusion has to survive scrutiny after it is delivered.
