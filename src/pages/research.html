Research & Development

A research agenda for trustworthy computational intelligence.

What we are investigating, and why.

BLKPVNTHR conducts research into computational intelligence that can show its work — connecting conclusions to evidence, to time, to provenance, and to independent verification. The agenda below describes open research directions and technical uncertainties, not established results.

Evidence-grounded computational intelligence

Active research

Investigating methods that tie each assertion to its supporting evidence, and that identify conclusions which are not sufficiently supported.

Information provenance & traceability

Active research

Investigating how to preserve source-level provenance end to end, so a conclusion carries a record of where it came from.

Temporal knowledge systems

Active research

Investigating how to judge whether retrieved evidence is current and temporally appropriate to the question being asked.

Independent assertion verification

Active research

Investigating independent, computational checks of candidate assertions rather than trusting a single model's self-assessment.

Uncertainty-aware reasoning

Active research

Investigating when a system should express uncertainty or abstain rather than assert a conclusion it cannot adequately support.

Governed institutional knowledge

Active research

Investigating governed knowledge boundaries so distinct institutional environments remain separate, with role-appropriate access.

Reproducible research

Active research

Investigating research records that let the same conclusion be regenerated from the same evidence, with assumptions intact.

Computational performance & scalability

Active research

Investigating whether these methods can remain computationally practical at institutional scale.

Flagship Research initiative

STRIX

Our flagship research initiative investigates how to generate independently verifiable, temporally relevant, evidence-supported, and reproducible conclusions — and when to abstain instead.
