01 Flagship research initiative

STRIX

Structured Traceability, Retrieval, and Intelligence eXtraction. STRIX is a research-stage computational intelligence platform investigating methods for generating independently verifiable, temporally relevant, evidence-supported, and reproducible conclusions.

Research motivation

Why existing methods fall short

Contemporary AI systems can produce plausible output without showing that their assertions are sufficiently supported, current, independently verifiable, or appropriate for a given institutional context. STRIX investigates that gap directly.

Proposed capabilities

What the research investigates

Integrating evidence quality, temporal validity, provenance, contradiction analysis, uncertainty, and independent verification into a single research and decision-support process — including the ability to abstain when support is insufficient.

Current status

Research and development

STRIX is under active research. The capabilities described here are research objectives and technical hypotheses, not validated performance claims. Advancement depends on documented experimental evidence.

02 Research & engineering capabilities

Trustworthy computational intelligence, built on a working engineering foundation.

Some of these are established engineering competencies the company practices today; others are capabilities currently under research. Each card is labeled so the distinction stays clear.

Evidence-grounded AI

Under research

Investigating methods that connect each assertion to supporting evidence and surface conclusions that lack sufficient support.

Independent verification

Under research

Investigating independent, computational checks of candidate assertions rather than relying on a single model's self-report.

Temporal intelligence

Under research

Investigating how to assess whether retrieved evidence is current and temporally appropriate to the question being asked.

Analytical engines & scenario modeling

Engineering capability

Python computation cores for simulation, projection, and quantitative analysis — deterministic, testable, and separable from any interface.

Data & knowledge infrastructure

Engineering capability

Connectors, ingestion pipelines, and documented APIs that bring external data into a model without hand assembly, surfacing schema and source failures early.

Institutional knowledge governance

Under research

Investigating governed knowledge boundaries so separate institutional environments can be kept distinct, with source-level provenance.

Reproducible research

Under research

Investigating research records that let the same conclusion be regenerated from the same evidence, with its assumptions intact.

Customer-controlled deployment

Engineering capability

Applications designed to be packaged for customer-hosted or isolated environments, with role-based access intended to keep sensitive data inside the customer's boundary.

Decision interfaces

Engineering capability

React dashboards and analyst tooling designed for interpretation — legible to non-technical decision makers, dense only where density helps.

05 From research to deployment

A disciplined research-to-commercialization path.

Advancement between stages depends on documented technical evidence and demonstrated commercial need — not on optimism.

  1. Stage 01 Research

    Research

    Define the scientific and engineering problem and the methods under investigation.

  2. Stage 02 Feasibility

    Technical feasibility

    Investigate whether the approach is technically practical at institutional scale.

  3. Stage 03 Validation

    Independent validation

    Evaluate results against conventional baselines with documented, independent methods.

  4. Stage 04 Demonstration

    Institutional demonstration

    Demonstrate across independently governed knowledge environments using authorized material.

  5. Stage 05 Discovery

    Customer discovery

    Gather documented evidence of real institutional need before building for the market.

  6. Stage 06 Deployment

    Commercial deployment

    Advance to supported deployment only after validation and demonstrated commercial need.
