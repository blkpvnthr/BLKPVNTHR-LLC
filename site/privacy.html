Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Scope

This policy describes how BLKPVNTHR LLC ("BLKPVNTHR", "we") handles information in connection with the public website at blkpvnthr.com. It does not cover separate products or services, which carry their own terms and privacy notices.

Information we collect on this site

This website is a static informational site. It contains no contact forms, no account system, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking scripts. We do not set cookies, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information through the pages themselves.

Web fonts

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those files, Google receives your IP address and standard request headers. That processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy and is outside our control. No other third-party resources are loaded.

Hosting

The site is served by a third-party static hosting provider, which may record standard server request logs (including IP addresses) for security and operational purposes in accordance with its own policies.

Email correspondence

If you email us, we receive the contents of your message and your email address. We use that information solely to respond to your inquiry and to maintain a record of the correspondence. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Retention

Email correspondence is retained for as long as it remains relevant to an ongoing or prospective business relationship, or as required for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us by emailing contact@blkpvnthr.com . Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law; we will honor valid requests to the extent the law requires.

Children

This site is directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
