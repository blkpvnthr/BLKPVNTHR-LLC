01 Capabilities

Software for complex decisions.

We build the analytical layer beneath consequential decisions — the engines that compute, the interfaces that explain, and the records that let a conclusion be defended months later.

01

Analytical engines

Python computation cores for simulation, projection, and quantitative analysis. Deterministic, testable, and separated from presentation so results can be verified independently of any interface.

02

Scenario planning

Systems for constructing, versioning, and comparing alternative futures. Assumptions are explicit and editable, so stakeholders can interrogate a result rather than accept it.

03

Decision interfaces

React dashboards and analyst tooling designed for interpretation, not decoration. Dense where density helps, restrained everywhere else, and legible to non-technical decision makers.

04

Data integration

Connectors, ingestion pipelines, and documented APIs that bring external data into a model without hand assembly. Schema changes and source failures surface early rather than silently corrupting output.

05

Evidence and auditability

Designed to attach provenance to every input, version every assumption, and regenerate the same figures from the same data, so a recommendation carries its supporting evidence rather than standing alone.

06

Customer-controlled deployment

Applications are designed to be packaged for customer-hosted or isolated environments, with role-based access and configuration intended to keep sensitive data inside the customer's own boundary.

03 Energy & Infrastructure / Technology Licensing

Exploring licensed technology commercialization.

BLKPVNTHR LLC is interested in discussing a test-and-evaluation pathway for ReactorCast, subject to Los Alamos National Laboratory's licensing requirements. BLKPVNTHR brings experience in analytical software engineering, Python computation systems, React decision interfaces, data integration, reproducible reporting, and customer-controlled deployment. Any commercialization effort would pair these capabilities with qualified nuclear-workforce and reactor-domain expertise.

Licensing status

BLKPVNTHR LLC has not licensed ReactorCast and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Los Alamos National Laboratory or the U.S. Department of Energy.

A disciplined path to commercialization

Each stage is designed to answer a specific technical, commercial, or domain-validation question before BLKPVNTHR advances to the next level of commitment.

  1. Stage 01 Evaluate

    Technical and licensing introduction

    Confirm ReactorCast's intended use, current maturity, available documentation, licensing framework, evaluation access, and commercialization expectations.

  2. Stage 02 Evaluate

    Controlled technical evaluation

    Assess the architecture, modeling assumptions, data requirements, extensibility, deployment constraints, maintainability, and reproducibility of representative outputs.

  3. Stage 03 Evaluate

    Domain and customer validation

    Engage qualified nuclear-workforce and reactor-domain advisors to evaluate the methodology, identify credible use cases, and conduct structured discovery with prospective users.

  4. Stage 04 Decide

    License and pilot decision

    Prepare a documented go/no-go business case covering licensing terms, technical findings, customer demand, required investment, domain-support needs, and a defined pilot opportunity.

  5. Stage 05 Commercialize

    Modernization pilot

    Subject to an appropriate license, develop a limited pilot with agreed use cases, success criteria, validated data, traceable assumptions, and domain-expert review.

  6. Stage 06 Commercialize

    Supported enterprise deployment

    Advance beyond the pilot only after analytical validation, user acceptance, security and deployment review, operating documentation, and a sustainable support model are established.

Multidisciplinary validation

BLKPVNTHR would contribute analytical software engineering, data integration, productization, and customer-controlled deployment capabilities. Qualified nuclear-workforce, energy-policy, and reactor-development advisors would participate throughout evaluation and pilot development. No resulting product would be represented as domain-validated before that review is completed.

06 Frequently asked

Questions we are asked.

What does BLKPVNTHR build?

Analytical engines, research platforms, dashboards, and decision-support systems. In practice that means Python computation cores, React interfaces, data integrations, and the reporting and audit layers that make results defensible.

Is ReactorCast currently a BLKPVNTHR product?

No. BLKPVNTHR LLC is interested in discussing a licensed test-and-evaluation pathway for ReactorCast. The company has not licensed it, does not own it, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Los Alamos National Laboratory or the U.S. Department of Energy.

Why is BLKPVNTHR evaluating energy-system software?

The engineering requirements overlap almost entirely with what the company already builds: scenario modeling, lifecycle-cost analysis, external-data integration, dashboards, and auditable reporting. The domain is different; the software problem is familiar.

Does BLKPVNTHR provide custom software services?

Yes, through PantherByte — BLKPVNTHR's productized engineering service line for fixed-scope websites, workflow automation, dashboards, integrations, technical diagnostics, and ongoing maintenance. Larger analytical or decision-support engagements are handled directly by BLKPVNTHR LLC.

Can BLKPVNTHR support customer-hosted deployments?

Yes. Applications are built to be packaged for a customer's own environment, with role-based access, audit history, and operational documentation. The company does not hold security certifications or clearances, and does not claim authorization to operate in classified environments.

How can a laboratory, company, or advisor start a conversation?

Email contact@blkpvnthr.com with a short description of the opportunity and the outcome you are trying to reach. Technical introductions, evaluation discussions, and advisor conversations are all welcome.
